Kituvo – Document Scanner

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kituvo – Document Scanner handles information when you use the application.

Kituvo – Document Scanner is designed with an offline-first approach. The application does not require you to create an account to use its document scanning and PDF creation features.

1. Information We Access

Kituvo – Document Scanner may request access to the following information or device features:

  • Camera: The camera is used when you choose to scan a document. Camera images are used to create scanned document pages.
  • Images selected by the user: When you choose to import an image from your device, the application accesses the image selected by you so that it can be processed as a document page.
  • Documents and PDF files: When you choose to open, create, save, share, or delete a PDF, the application processes the files required to perform the action requested by you.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information and files accessed by Kituvo – Document Scanner are used only to provide the application's core functionality, including:

  • Scanning documents.
  • Processing and adjusting scanned pages.
  • Rotating and cropping document pages.
  • Creating PDF documents.
  • Opening PDF documents.
  • Saving documents locally.
  • Renaming and deleting saved documents.
  • Sharing documents when you explicitly choose to do so.

3. Local Storage

Documents created by the application may be stored locally on your device so that you can access them through the application's document library.

You can delete saved documents using the application's delete functionality.

4. Data Collection and Sharing

Kituvo – Document Scanner does not require an account and does not intentionally collect your scanned document content, photos, or PDFs for its own remote storage.

Kituvo does not sell your personal information.

When you explicitly use Android's sharing functionality, the file you choose to share may be transferred to the application or service you select. That transfer is controlled by your action and is subject to the privacy policy of the receiving application or service.

5. Internet and Third-Party Services

The core document scanning and PDF creation functionality is designed to work offline.

Some Android and Google system components used by the application may communicate with their respective services as required by the Android operating system or those components. Such services are governed by the privacy practices of their respective providers.

6. Data Security

Kituvo – Document Scanner is designed to process documents locally whenever possible. We do not intentionally transmit your scanned documents to our own servers for storage.

Users should also protect access to their device using the security features provided by their operating system.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Documents saved by Kituvo remain on the device until you delete them, uninstall the application, or otherwise remove the files using the available device functionality.

You can delete saved PDF documents directly from the application's document library.

Because Kituvo does not provide user accounts, there is no Kituvo account or remote account data to delete.

8. Children's Privacy

Kituvo – Document Scanner is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application or its data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
